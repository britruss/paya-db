### Macro & Calorie Estimation Notes

Please note that all nutritional values and macronutrient counts are ballpark estimates. 

**Rounding Guidelines:**
* **Values under 1g:** Typically rounded down to 0g.
* **Protein:** Generally rounded down to the nearest whole number.
* **Fats & Carbohydrates:** Generally rounded up to the nearest whole number.
* **Exceptions:** In certain instances, standard mathematical rounding was applied instead of the rules above.

**Calorie Calculations & Variances:**
* Stated calorie totals may not perfectly align with the sum of the individual rounded macros (4 kcal/g for protein and carbs, 9 kcal/g for fats).
* Data was entered, calculated, and adjusted manually on different days and at different times, so slight discrepancies may be present.
